# Privacy Policy — NKF (NightKnight Files)

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

**NKF (NightKnight Files)** is a 100% offline, free and open-source (MIT) file manager.

## Data Collection
- **Zero telemetry:** NKF does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, crash reports, file names, or file contents.
- **100% local processing:** All file browsing, search, ZIP compression/extraction, SHA-256 hashing, duplicate detection, and side-by-side file editing happen strictly on your local device.
- **Local preferences only:** Your chosen theme, bookmarks, recent folders, and saved network share shortcuts are stored locally on your machine and never leave your device.
- **External links:** Clicking the optional "Support" button opens your default web browser to our external Buy Me a Coffee page (`https://buymeacoffee.com/aurorauap`). No data is sent automatically.
